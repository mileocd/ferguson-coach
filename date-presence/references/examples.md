# Presence examples

## Quiet café, nervous user
Prepare one real story, normal comfortable clothing, and the route. On the date, follow a detail, contribute, and pause. A quiet personality can be engaging without becoming loud.

## Loud venue
Move to a place where both can hear if possible. Leaning closer to hear is ordinary; check comfort and leave space. Do not call proximity a dominance technique.

## Date is sharing something important
Stop looking for a joke. “That sounds important to you—what was it like?” is more fitting than an absurd reinterpretation. Ask naturally rather than perform a counseling session.

## No obvious conversational spark
Notice something in the surroundings or return to a real earlier topic. If neither is enjoying the exchange, a polite end is valid. Do not prescribe escalating touch.

## Wants to kiss but is unsure
If the user wants this and the interaction supports asking: “I'd like to kiss you. Would you like that?”
Accept no, hesitation, or a preference to slow down. The skill cannot establish consent from a described smile.

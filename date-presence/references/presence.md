# Date presence

Basis: the shared techniques reference T04-T08 and practical coaching judgment. Physical preparation is practical coaching, not evidence that a posture or pitch creates attraction.

Before: confirm practical details, choose clothes appropriate to the activity and comfortable to wear, attend to ordinary grooming, plan travel, and avoid rushing. Prepare one or two true stories, not a memorized interview. The useful mindset is curiosity about mutual fit.

Just before meeting: settle breathing naturally, loosen unnecessary shoulder and jaw tension, put the phone away, and notice the surroundings. These are optional grounding cues, not a treatment protocol.

During: sit or stand comfortably with room for both people to move. Use natural eye contact, including looking away; do not force gaze or mirror mechanically. Speak audibly in your normal voice. If rushing, finish one thought and leave a pause. Leaning in to hear in a noisy room is not a sign of low status. Move back if the other person wants space.

Conversation: ask about what caught your interest, contribute a real piece of yourself, and follow what they emphasize. Use a small shared observation when it fits; stop trying to be funny when sincerity is needed. A date does not require constant performance.

Physical affection: mutual willingness matters. Friendliness, laughter, silence, or a smile do not settle consent. If uncertain, ask clearly and comfortably; accept no or hesitation without trying to change it.

After: record one moment of ease, one mismatch or uncertainty, and one skill to practice. Judge progress by presence and honest connection, not only by obtaining a second date.

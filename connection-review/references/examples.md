# Review examples

## Good conversation, unclear future
Facts: two hours talking, both laughed, no second date discussed.
Interpretation: enjoyable time together; future interest is unknown.
If the user wants another date, a direct follow-up can provide clarity.

## Similar humor, conflicting intentions
The user wants a relationship; the match explicitly wants casual dating.
Shared jokes do not cancel that mismatch. Help state intentions honestly or step back.

## Warm words, little follow-through
Repeated vague plans without arranging anything.
Describe the pattern without assigning a motive. The user may choose to stop investing even without proving why it happens.

## User wants to improve
They interrupted several times because they were excited.
Practice waiting until the person finishes and following one detail before contributing. Do not frame this as a character flaw.

## User wants to end kindly
Use an honest short close. Do not invent a busy schedule or promise future contact to soften the message.
